# Facility Stands (CRUD)

## Overview

Each facility has up to 4 stands. Besides the merge done by `PUT /api/v{version}/facilities/{id}` ([`facility-update.md`](./facility-update.md)), stands have their own endpoints under `/api/v{version}/facilities/{facilityId}/stands`: list, read, create, replace and delete. A stand always belongs to one facility; addressing it through a different facility is treated as "not found".

### Who it is for

- Front-end and integration teams that edit the stands of a facility one at a time.
- Any consumer of `Gaffer.Api.Client` (the client is regenerated and versioned by the operator).

### Use cases

- List the stands of a facility (empty list when there are none).
- Read one stand.
- Add a stand to a facility with fewer than 4 stands.
- Rename a stand or change its capacity.
- Delete a stand, including the last one.

### Behaviour

| Operation | Request | Success | Errors |
|---|---|---|---|
| List | `GET .../facilities/{facilityId}/stands` | `200`, list of stands | `404` `Error.Facilities.Stands.FacilityNotFound` |
| Read | `GET .../stands/{standId}` | `200`, the stand | `404` `Error.Facilities.Stands.FacilityNotFound` or `Error.Facilities.Stands.StandNotFound` |
| Create | `POST .../stands` with `{ standName, standCapacity }` | `201`, body is the new stand id, `Location` points to the stand | `404` facility not found; `400` `Error.Facilities.Stands.Validation` for invalid fields; `400` `Error.Facilities.Stands.Create.MaxStandsExceeded` when the facility already has 4 |
| Replace | `PUT .../stands/{standId}` with `{ standName, standCapacity }` | `204` | `404` facility or stand not found; `400` `Error.Facilities.Stands.Validation` for invalid fields |
| Delete | `DELETE .../stands/{standId}` | `204` | `404` facility or stand not found |

- The id of a new stand is always generated by the server; an `id` sent in the body is ignored.
- Replacing a stand keeps its id.
- A stand that does not exist, or that belongs to another facility, answers `404` `Error.Facilities.Stands.StandNotFound` and is never changed.
- Create, replace and delete lock the facility while they run, so concurrent requests on the same facility are serialized and the 4-stand limit holds.

### Limitations

- `Delete(facilityId)` of a whole facility does not take that lock (backlog item R4-1 of TECHDEBT-004), so deleting a facility while its stands are being changed is not serialized.
- `standName` must be 1-100 characters and not only whitespace, and `standCapacity` must be 0 or more; both operations (create and replace) check the body before looking up the facility, so an invalid body answers `400` even for an unknown facility. Errors list the fields `StandName` and `StandCapacity`; format and rules: [`input-validation.md`](./input-validation.md). Stands already stored are not revalidated. There is no uniqueness of names and no rule on the sum of capacities.
- Replace and delete return an empty body.
